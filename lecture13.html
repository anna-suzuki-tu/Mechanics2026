Lecture 12 — Analytical Mechanics II: Lagrange Equations

You derived and applied the Euler-Lagrange equation, identified cyclic coordinates and their conservation laws, and applied the Lagrangian method to multi-DOF systems including the cart-pendulum and rolling cylinder. You saw how constraint forces disappear automatically and how n degrees of freedom yield n independent equations of motion.

Session Overview

This consolidation session covers Lectures 11 and 12. For each problem you will: (1) identify the most appropriate solution method and explain why, (2) apply the Euler-Lagrange method to derive the equation of motion, and (3) compare with Newton's method and articulate what disappeared.

How This Session Works
  1. Attempt each problem on paper before revealing the answer Work through the problem yourself first. Click Show Answer only after attempting. The learning happens in the attempt — not in reading the solution passively.
  2. Compare your reasoning, not just your final answer After revealing the answer, check whether your steps and reasoning match. Pay attention to which forces disappeared and at what point — this is the key insight of analytical mechanics.
  3. Use this session to prepare for the final exam No report is required. Work through as many problems as you can at your own pace. Focus on being able to explain why a particular method works — and what disappears — rather than just reproducing the answer.
