Lecture 8 — Kinetics of Rigid Bodies

You applied Newton's laws to rigid bodies: ΣF = m·a_G for translation and ΣM_G = I_G·α for rotation. You handled rolling without slip using the kinematic constraint a_G = R·α, and applied the parallel axis theorem to find I_O for pinned bodies.

Learning Goals for This Lecture
  1. Express total kinetic energy of a rigid body as translation plus rotation T = ½m·v_G² + ½I_G·ω². Both terms are always present for general plane motion. For pure rotation about a fixed point use T = ½I_O·ω². Rolling friction does zero work — the contact point has zero velocity.
  2. Calculate work done by moments and couples Work done by a constant couple M rotating through angle θ: U = M·θ. This is the rotational analogue of W = F·d. For rolling bodies, friction at the contact point does zero work.
  3. Apply angular momentum conservation to rigid body collisions Angular momentum H_G is conserved when ΣM_G = 0. For eccentric impact (off-center collision), combine: linear momentum conservation + angular momentum conservation about the impact point + the coefficient of restitution equation.
Caution ⚠

Friction in rolling without slip does zero work — do not subtract a friction work term from the energy equation. Angular momentum conservation requires zero net external moment, not zero net external force. Eccentric impact needs three equations, not two — missing the angular equation is the most common error.

🟢 Basic Problems — Explained in Main Video
Basic 1
Total Kinetic Energy of Rolling
A uniform disk (mass m, radius R, I_G = ½mR²) rolls without slipping with center velocity v_G. Write the total kinetic energy T and simplify using the rolling constraint v_G = R·ω.
Basic 2
Angular Momentum Conservation
A flywheel (M = 20 kg, R = 0.5 m, I_G = ½MR²) spins at ω₁ = 10 rad/s. A mass m = 5 kg is gently placed on its rim (no slip). Find the new angular velocity ω₂.
🟡 Standard Problems — Core
Standard 1
Rolling Ball up a Ramp
A solid sphere (m = 3 kg, R = 0.15 m, I_G = ⅖mR²) rolls at v₀ = 6 m/s on a flat surface and meets a 20° rough incline. Use energy conservation to find the maximum height h it reaches.
Standard 2
Eccentric Impact on a Rod
A uniform rod (mass M, length L, I_O = ⅓ML²) is pinned at O and at rest. A ball (mass m, velocity v₀) hits the rod at distance d from O and sticks (e = 0). Find ω after impact and the impulse at pin O. Hint: linear momentum is NOT conserved, but angular momentum about O is.
Standard 3
Sudden Loading of a Flywheel
A flywheel (M = 20 kg, R = 0.5 m, I_G = ½MR²) spins at ω₁ = 10 rad/s. A mass m = 5 kg is gently placed on its rim. Find ω₂ and the kinetic energy lost.
🔴 Advanced Problem — Optional
Advanced 1
Door Impact — Full Analysis
A door (M = 30 kg, L = 1.0 m, I_O = ⅓ML²) is vertical and at rest. A ball (m = 2 kg, v₀ = 30 m/s) hits the free edge and embeds (e = 0). (1) Find ω just after impact. (2) Find the horizontal hinge impulse at O. (3) Determine if the door swings to horizontal using an energy check.
