Lecture 2 — Curvilinear Motion & Relative Motion

You learned T–N coordinates: a_t = dv/dt for speed change, a_n = v²/ρ for direction change. Projectile motion was solved by treating x and y independently. Relative velocity was handled by vector subtraction v_B/A = v_B − v_A.

Learning Goals for This Lecture
  1. Draw correct Free Body Diagrams (FBD) An FBD contains only real forces acting on the body — weight, normal force, friction, tension. Never include ma (the result) as a force on the diagram. This is the most common conceptual error.
  2. Choose a coordinate system aligned with the motion For inclined planes, align one axis along the slope. For circular motion, use normal-tangential coordinates. The right axis choice reduces the number of equation terms by half.
  3. Apply Newton's second law systematically in each direction Write ΣF = ma for each independent direction. For curved paths, the normal equation gives ΣF_n = mv²/ρ. Always resolve all forces into the chosen coordinate system before writing equations.
Caution ⚠

Never put ma on the FBD — it is the net effect of forces, not a force itself. Static friction is not always μ_s·N; it takes only the value needed for equilibrium, up to that maximum. Always check whether the object is moving before choosing kinetic vs. static friction.

🟢 Basic Problems — Explained in Main Video
Basic 1
FBD on a Frictionless Surface
A 5 kg block sits on a frictionless horizontal surface. A horizontal force F = 20 N is applied to the right. Draw the complete FBD: identify every force, its direction, and its source. Then find the acceleration.
Basic 2
FBD with Kinetic Friction
A 10 kg block on a horizontal surface (μ_k = 0.3) is pulled by a horizontal force F = 40 N. The block is moving. Draw the FBD, write ΣF = ma in the x-direction, and find the acceleration.
🟡 Standard Problems — Core
Standard 1
Block on an Incline with Friction
A 20 kg block slides down a 30° incline with μ_k = 0.2. Find the acceleration. Hint: align one axis parallel to the incline, one perpendicular. Write ΣF = ma along each axis separately.
Standard 2
Atwood Machine
Two masses m₁ = 5 kg and m₂ = 8 kg are connected by a rope over a massless frictionless pulley. Find the acceleration of the system and the tension in the rope. Hint: draw separate FBDs for each mass; they share the same |a| and T.
Standard 3
Circular Motion — String Tension
A 2 kg ball on a 1.5 m string moves in a horizontal circle at constant speed v = 4 m/s. Find the tension in the string. Hint: ΣF_n = mv²/ρ directed toward the center.
🔴 Advanced Problem — Optional
Advanced 1
Coupled Blocks — No-Slip Condition
Block A (3 kg) sits on top of block B (7 kg). A horizontal force F is applied to block B. The contact surface between A and B has μ_s = 0.4; the floor below B is frictionless. Find the maximum force F such that A and B move together without A slipping on B. What happens if F exceeds this value?
